I'm Lloyd Watkin. I build web applications for a living, and spend the rest of my time on trails or in the workshop.

By day I'm the principal engineer at [Olio](https://olioapp.com), an app that connects neighbours and local businesses so surplus food and household items get shared rather than thrown away.

This site is where I write about AI, coding, running and DIY projects, share 3D models I've designed, and keep a few small tools that run in the browser. The [talks](/talks/) section has slides from conference talks I gave about XMPP and realtime web apps.

You can find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/lloydwatkin), photos on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lloydwatkin/), and my work history on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lloydwatkin/).
