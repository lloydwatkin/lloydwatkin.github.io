I started logging runs on Strava in April 2020, ran my first race in late 2021, and since then I've been lucky enough to toe the line at some properly special events — from a Christmas ultra in the Welsh valleys to the Sahara. Here's the full list, warts, DNFs and all.

Distances, ascent and times come from my own watch (elapsed time), so they won't always match the official results exactly.
