A jewellery box I printed for my daughter. The viewer shows the base, with a large compartment, a front tray and a 3 × 4 grid of cubbies for stud earrings. The lid has a lip that sits inside the base with 0.25 mm of clearance on each side.

The other parts:

- [Lid (STL)](/models/horse-jewellery-box-lid.stl), already flipped so the decorated face prints on the plate.
- [Lid inlay (STL)](/models/horse-jewellery-box-lid-inlay.stl), the horse head and border for a second colour.
- [Two-colour lid (3MF)](/models/horse-jewellery-box-lid-2colour.3mf), the lid and inlay together for a multi-colour printer.
